Goal: collect pets and earn money.
  1. Everyone plays on the same server (top right) - one pen each. When all pens are taken, a new server opens for the next player.
  2. Walk into the yellow Throw Zone and throw an egg with Space / Click (touch: Throw). You can only throw forward, towards the islands.
  3. Land, then get close to an egg and click it to steal it. Run out of the area before the chicken (or the scorpion in the Sands) catches you!
  4. Next to another player? Press G twice (or the Gift button on a phone) to give them the egg you are carrying (after you escaped with it) or, with empty hands, the pet selected in your hotbar. Press H (or Gift pet on a phone) to gift the selected pet even while you carry an egg. Pets standing in your pen: click one to take it back into your hotbar first. Needs multiplayer.
  5. Take it to your pen and click a spot to place it. A timer counts down until it hatches (bigger egg = longer).
  6. The pet lands in your hotbar. Pick it with 1-9 or the mouse wheel, then click a spot in your pen to place it. Pets in your pen earn money every second (chicken $10/s up to snake $50/s, camel $60/s and scorpion $75/s at 1x, more for bigger eggs).
  7. In your pen, click the Pull-Up Bar to gain Strength (throws go farther) and click the Treadmill to gain Speed. You hop on automatically and keep training on your own; click again to stop.
  8. Click a pet standing in your pen to take it back into your hotbar. Your pen holds 5 pets to start.
  9. Sign in (email or Google) and your money, pets, pen, upgrades and training are saved. Guests are not saved.
  10. Open Upgrades (button on the left, or press U) to upgrade the Pull-Up Bar, the Treadmill, and your pen (+1 pet slot each).
  11. Open the Index button (or press I) to see every pet.
  12. Press Enter (touch: the chat button) to chat with the other players on your server.
