Throw An Egg

Loading...

Goal: collect pets and earn money.
  1. Walk into the yellow Throw Zone and throw an egg with Space / Click (touch: Throw).
  2. Land, then get close to an egg and click it to steal it. Run out of the area before the chicken (or the scorpion in the Sands) catches you!
  3. Take it to your pen and click a spot to place it. A timer counts down until it hatches (bigger egg = longer).
  4. The pet lands in your hotbar. Pick it with 1-8 or the mouse wheel, then click a spot in your pen to place it. Pets in your pen earn money every second (chicken $10/s, bunny and ladybug $25/s at 1x, more for bigger eggs).
  5. In your pen, click the Pull-Up Bar to gain Strength (throws go farther) and click the Treadmill to gain Speed. You hop on automatically and keep training on your own; click again to stop.
  6. Open the Index button (or press I) to see every pet.
