TAE Test Map
============
Run:   run.bat (Windows) or ./run.sh (Mac/Linux), then open http://localhost:8000
       (Do not double-click index.html; browsers block GLB loading from file://)

Controls: WASD move, Mouse look, Shift run, Space jump, R respawn
Throw:    Enter the yellow Throw Zone. Power bar (5-20) appears next to the player.
          Space or Click stops it and throws the egg.
URL opts: ?debug=1  ?verbose=1  ?spawn=N
